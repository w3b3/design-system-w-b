01 / Color

White paper. One accent. Black ink.

White dominates. The accent is the only color — CTAs, line-art, construction marks. Black ink carries text and structure. Use it with conviction but sparingly.

03 / Buttons

Solid. Sharp. Offset.

Hard corners, ink border, solid offset shadow. Hover lifts translate(-2px,-2px); press sinks translate(3px,3px). Accent fill = primary, white = ghost.

04 / Cards & Shadows

6px 6px 0 in accent or ink. Ink cards punctuate sections and pricing blocks.

05 / Forms & Inputs

Crisp fields. Hard corners.

Labels are Space Mono uppercase. 2.5px ink borders, no radius. Focus switches the border to the accent, with no glow.

08 / Flagship theme

tatame0. Where Blueprint came from.

Blueprint was extracted from the tatame0 site, inspired by gym mats and engineering drawings. Its theme file sets Tatame Red and adds a domain palette, the IBJJF youth and adult belts. A project theme can add its own palette the same way, without touching the core tokens.

09 / Use it

Three links and you're on-system.

Load the fonts, the core tokens and one theme. To use your own accent, override the four --t-accent-* variables. The picker at the top gives you the values to copy. Everything else follows.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Big+Shoulders+Display:wght@700;800;900&family=Space+Mono:wght@400;700&family=Archivo:wght@400;500;600;700;800;900&display=swap">
<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://design.w-b.dev/tokens.css">
<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://design.w-b.dev/themes/tatame0.css">
